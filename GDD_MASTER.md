# 🎮 GLOBAL SYNDICATE — Master Game Design Document (GDD)

## 1. Executive Summary
- **Title:** Global Syndicate
- **Genre:** Photorealistic AAA Open-World / Geopolitical Syndicate Simulation
- **Platform Strategy:** Mobile-First Design & Social Integration, Laptop/PC Execution (Godot Engine)
- **Monetization & Economy:** Automated player tax system, custom vehicle marketplace, regional vault heists

---

## 2. World Pillars & Architecture
1. **The Apex Towers:** Single 1,000,000-unit indestructible tower per nation controlled by **The Sovereign**.
2. **Undersea Transit Network (UTN):** Pressurized ocean tubes linking national grids with 450 KPH speed boosters.
3. **5-Tier Syndicate Hierarchy:** Associate ➔ Operator ➔ Captain ➔ Regional Executive ➔ Sovereign.

---

## 3. Core Mechanics & Technical Stack
- **Database Layer:** Supabase (PostgreSQL with custom RPC triggers for tax distribution).
- **Backend API:** Node.js / Express REST API for vault validation and transit gate permissions.
- **Client Engine:** Godot Engine (GDScript for vehicle physics and UTN tube interactions).

---

## 4. Viral Social Strategy (9:16 Vertical)
- **The Sovereign:** Flexing national dominion, Apex Tower control, tax revenues.
- **Official Handle:** Mechanics devlogs, 450 KPH UTN runs, vehicle engine revs.
- **Community Hub:** Squad heist fails, vault payouts, user-generated content.
